## 摘要
本文提出马尔可夫链蒙特卡洛（MCMC）循环的概念，它类比于热机中的循环过程，用于考察贝叶斯推断问题。为此，我们开发了自适应系综调度器，可在 MCMC 运行过程中调节贝叶斯正则系综的外部参数，从而在实践中实现 MCMC 循环。我们在不同的统计模型上运行这些循环。作为一个基本洞见，我们发现（在理论和实践中均如此）此类系统能够产生非零净功输出，当且仅当所考虑的模型为非高斯模型。因此，它们可作为贝叶斯推断中非高斯性的一种度量，我们并在超新星宇宙学的一个例子中对其进行了检验。

## Abstract
The concept of Markov chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) cycles, an analogy to cyclic processes in heat engines, is presented in order to examine Bayesian inference problems. In this effort, we develop adaptive ensemble schedulers that allow the tuning of external parameters of a Bayesian canonical ensemble during an MCMC run, realising the MCMC cycles in practice. We run these cycles on different statistical models. As a fundamental insight, we find (both theoretically and in practice) that such systems can produce a non-zero net work output if and only if the considered model is non-Gaussian. As such, they may serve as a measure of non-Gaussianity in Bayesian inference, which we test on an example from supernova cosmology.
