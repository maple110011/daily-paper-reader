## 摘要
我们引入了一种针对多水平高斯贝叶斯结构方程模型（SEM）的闭式、无需重拟合的留一簇（LOCO）交叉验证程序，以及对每个嵌套子模型的预测评分。给定参数时，簇的条件独立性将删除簇后的后验表达为完整后验的泛函。LOCO预测密度因此是簇似然的调和平均，可通过INLAvaan（用于贝叶斯SEM的集成嵌套拉普拉斯近似包）中的单次拟合计算得出。我们通过在高斯后验下对倒数簇似然进行完全指数拉普拉斯近似，以闭式形式评估调和平均期望；候选结构限制通过高斯条件化相同拉普拉斯汇总得出。由此产生的Taylor elpd（期望对数预测密度）评分完全确定性，既不需要重拟合也不需要蒙特卡洛抽样，因此朴素调和平均估计量的方差病理不会出现。复合对称簇协方差的直和分解使成本独立于簇大小，比暴力重拟合低数个数量级。我们在模拟中针对暴力重拟合和马尔可夫链蒙特卡洛进行了验证，并说明了该方法在PISA 2022学校安全氛围以及MIDUS兄弟姐妹样本中人格与幸福感关联的16个候选结构上的应用。

## Abstract
We introduce a closed-form, refit-free procedure for leave-one-cluster-out (LOCO) cross-validation in multilevel Gaussian Bayesian structural equation models (SEMs), together with predictive scoring of every nested submodel. Conditional independence of clusters given the parameters expresses the cluster-deleted posterior as a functional of the full posterior. The LOCO predictive density is then a harmonic mean of the cluster likelihood, computable from a single fit in INLAvaan, the integrated nested Laplace approximation package for Bayesian SEM. We evaluate the harmonic-mean expectation in closed form through a fully exponential Laplace approximation of the reciprocal cluster likelihood under a Gaussian posterior; candidate structural restrictions follow by Gaussian conditioning of the same Laplace summary. The resulting Taylor elpd (expected log predictive density) scores are fully deterministic, requiring neither refitting nor Monte Carlo sampling, so the variance pathology of the naive harmonic-mean estimator does not arise. A direct-sum decomposition of the compound-symmetric cluster covariance makes the cost independent of cluster size, orders of magnitude below brute-force refitting. We validate against brute-force refits and Markov chain Monte Carlo in simulation, and illustrate the procedure on school-safety climate in PISA 2022 and on 16 candidate structures linking personality and well-being in the MIDUS sibling sample.
