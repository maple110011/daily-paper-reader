## 摘要
吉布斯采样和坐标上升变分推断（CAVI）是两种基本的基于坐标的统计计算方法。最近在强对数凹性假设下的分析，为这些算法中每一步更新一个均匀随机选择的块的版本建立了收敛速率。我们将这两个结果推广到任意固定的、严格正的选择概率。其速率由一个适应选择的凸性常数 $\lambda^{\star}_\theta$ 控制，该常数使用块平滑常数和选择概率 $\theta$ 定义。同样的常数也可以得到吉布斯采样器相对熵的收缩以及随机扫描CAVI平均场目标间隙的收缩。新的界恢复了均匀扫描的结果，并且绝不弱于基于最小选择概率的朴素比较。它们提供了一种利用曲率信息使扫描适应异构块几何结构的原则性方法。

## Abstract
Gibbs sampling and coordinate ascent variational inference (CAVI) are two basic coordinate-wise methods for statistical computation. Recent analyses under strong log-concavity establish convergence rates for versions of these algorithms that update one uniformly selected block at each step. We extend both results to arbitrary fixed, strictly positive selection probabilities. The rates are governed by a selection-adapted convexity constant $λ^{\star}_θ$, defined using the block-smoothness constants and the selection probabilities $θ$. The same constant yields a contraction of relative entropy for the Gibbs sampler and a contraction of the mean-field objective gap for random-scan CAVI. The new bounds recover the uniform-scan results, and are never weaker than the naive comparison based on the smallest selection probability. They provide a principled way to adapt the scan to heterogeneous block geometry using curvature information.
