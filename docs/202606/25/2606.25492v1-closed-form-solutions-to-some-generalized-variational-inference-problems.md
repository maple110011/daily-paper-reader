## 摘要
Donsker--Varadhan公式将普通贝叶斯后验刻画为无限制KL正则化变分问题的解。广义变分推断用其他散度代替该正则化器，但由此产生的测度值优化问题通常仅在限制到参数变分族后才被研究。本文研究了无限制的测度层次问题。给定可测空间$(\mathcal{Z},\mathfrak{Z})$，先验概率测度$P$，可测损失$\ell:\mathcal{Z}\to(-\infty,\infty]$，正则化强度$α>0$，以及散度$\mathsf{D}(Q\Vert P)$，我们寻求概率测度使得\[ \underset{Q\in\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{Z})}{\mathrm{arg\,min}}\left\{\int_{\mathcal{Z}} \ell\,\mathrm{d}Q+α\mathsf{D}(Q\Vert P)\right\}. \] 对于$f$-散度惩罚，我们推导出标量逆梯度密度公式和一维对偶恒等式；以Kullback--Leibler、Cressie--Read和平方Hellinger问题为例。逆向$f$-散度和混合前向/逆向Kullback--Leibler惩罚遵循相同的可分离积分原理。对于密度之间的Bregman散度，我们获得了具有标量质量乘子的密度空间解，包括最小二乘、密度幂和Burg/Itakura--Saito示例。对于阶数$r>1$的Rényi惩罚，我们推导出归一化截断幂刻画和每个全局优化器的阈值方程。有限模型权重公式和简单的共轭贝叶斯模型示例展示了这些闭式形式如何在实践中实现，并与传统解决方案有何不同。

## Abstract
The Donsker--Varadhan formula characterizes the ordinary Bayesian posterior as the solution of an unrestricted $\mathsf{KL}$-regularized variational problem. Generalized variational inference replaces this regularizer by other divergences, but the resulting measure-valued optimization problem is often studied only after restriction to a parametric variational family. This paper studies the unrestricted measure-level problem. Given a measurable space $(\mathcal{Z},\mathfrak{Z})$, a prior probability measure $P$, a measurable loss $\ell:\mathcal{Z}\to(-\infty,\infty]$, a regularization strength $α>0$, and a divergence $\mathsf{D}(Q\Vert P)$, we seek probability measures in \[ \underset{Q\in\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{Z})}{\mathrm{arg\,min}}\left\{\int_{\mathcal{Z}} \ell\,\mathrm{d}Q+α\mathsf{D}(Q\Vert P)\right\}. \] For $f$-divergence penalties we derive a scalar inverse-gradient density formula and a one-dimensional dual identity; the Kullback--Leibler, Cressie--Read, and squared-Hellinger problems are treated as examples. Reverse $f$-divergences and mixed forward/reverse Kullback--Leibler penalties follow from the same separable integral principle. For Bregman divergences between densities we obtain a density-space solution with a scalar mass multiplier, including least-squares, density-power, and Burg/Itakura--Saito examples. For Rényi penalties of order $r>1$ we derive a normalized truncated-power characterization and a threshold equation for every global optimizer. Finite model-weight formulas and simple conjugate Bayesian model illustrations show how these closed forms are realized in practice and differ from the traditional solutions.
