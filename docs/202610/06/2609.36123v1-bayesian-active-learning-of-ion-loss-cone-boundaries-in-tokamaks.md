## 摘要
将托卡马克中损失锥边界的确定问题建模为一个主动学习问题：以具有径向基函数特征的贝叶斯逻辑回归模型作为学习器，以导心轨道积分器作为标注方法。该方法被应用于一个解析托卡马克平衡，在发射位置网格上进行，生成一个带有近似后验不确定性的损失概率模型数据库，其中每个模型均在规定的轨迹预算内生成。数据库生成后，可支持下游应用；本文以三个例子加以说明。第一，将概率模型对麦克斯韦分布进行积分，可得到损失离子份额以及损失离子携带的能量和平行动量，并将后验不确定性解析地传播到每个积分量。第二，由所得轨道损失力矩与径向角动量扩散之间的稳态平衡，给出与电流同向的边缘旋转，其幅度主要由规定的动量扩散率和损失区更新率决定。第三，在数据库上训练的神经网络代理模型学习从发射位置到损失概率图像的域内映射，并且由其预测计算得到的输运积分在同一网格的留出样本上能很好地重现贝叶斯参考值。

## Abstract
Loss-cone boundary determination in tokamaks is cast as an active learning problem in which a Bayesian logistic regression model with radial basis function features acts as the learner and the guiding-center orbit integrator acts as the labeling method. The approach is applied to an analytic tokamak equilibrium across a mesh of launch positions, producing a database of loss-probability models with approximate posterior uncertainty, each generated within a prescribed trajectory budget. Once generated, the database supports downstream applications, illustrated here with three examples. First, integrating the probability models against a Maxwellian distribution yields the lost-ion fraction and the energy and parallel momentum carried by lost ions, with posterior uncertainty propagated analytically to each integral. Second, a steady balance between the resulting orbit-loss torque and radial angular-momentum diffusion gives co-current edge rotation whose amplitude is set mainly by the prescribed momentum diffusivity and loss-region renewal rate. Third, a neural network surrogate trained on the database learns the in-domain mapping from launch position to loss-probability image, and transport integrals computed from its predictions closely reproduce the Bayesian reference values on held-out samples from the same mesh.
