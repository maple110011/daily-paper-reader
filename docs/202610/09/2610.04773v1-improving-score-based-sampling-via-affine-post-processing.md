## 摘要
仅能获取概率分布的对数密度时，从该分布中采样是统计学、机器学习和物理科学中的一个基础问题。该问题在无数据基于分数的扩散文献中得到研究，这些文献通过估计与高斯随机变量卷积所得密度的分数，并实现反向 SDE，来对目标密度进行采样。在此框架中，分数估计器通过马尔可夫链蒙特卡洛、重要性采样或拒绝采样构建。这些估计器通常运行在远超可建立混合时间与误差控制的范围，并且在估计潜在分数时具有较大的偏差和方差。在我们的方法中，我们提出后处理：将固定的计算预算分配到多次评估中，在不同点查询估计器，并通过线性组合拟合一个单一的汇总估计，以最小化目标位置处的偏差和方差。最终值是多个分数估计构成的向量的仿射映射。我们表明，与在单点查询且计算量匹配的基础估计器相比，这降低了分数估计误差。在诸如具有大条件数的对数凹密度、多峰密度和高维密度等困难目标密度上，我们证明其样本质量优于其他计算成本匹配的分数估计方法。

## Abstract
Sampling from a probability distribution with access only to its log-density is a foundational problem in statistics, machine learning, and the physical sciences. This problem is studied in the data-free score-based diffusion literature, which samples the target density via estimating scores of densities produced by convolution with Gaussian random variables and implementing a reverse SDE. In this framework, score estimators are constructed via Markov chain Monte Carlo, importance sampling, or rejection sampling. The estimators often operate far beyond the regime where mixing time and error control can be established and have large bias and variance when estimating the underlying score. In our method we propose post-processing: splitting a fixed computation budget across multiple evaluations, querying the estimator at different points, and fitting a single pooled estimate via linear combination to minimize bias and variance at a target location. The final value is an affine map of a vector of multiple score estimates. We show this reduces score estimation error when compared to the computation-matched base estimator queried at a single point. We demonstrate improved sample quality over other score estimation methods matched for computation cost on difficult target densities such as log-concave densities with large condition number, multimodal densities, and high-dimensional densities.
