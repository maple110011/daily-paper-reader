## 摘要
精确宇宙学是现代天文学的基石，而即将开展的宽视场测光巡天使得开发稳健的、数据驱动的推断方法至关重要，这些方法能够处理复杂的巡天系统误差，而不依赖于闭式似然。基于模拟的推断（SBI）通过支持能够编码复杂巡天特征的前向模拟来满足这一需求。此前超新星宇宙学中的SBI分析使用SALT2光变曲线参数作为摘要统计量；然而，SALT2的双基底光谱模板强加了刻板的建模假设，促使人们寻求更灵活的表示。在本工作中，我们首次将函数型主成分分析（FPCA）光变曲线参数作为摘要统计量，应用于非平坦ΛCDM模型下基于SBI的宇宙学推断。在相同生成的模拟数据上，FPCA+SBI给出的约束与基于SALT2的SBI和显式似然分析相当，同时在域外模拟上比SALT2提供更稳健的约束。将基于类LSST光变曲线训练的模型应用于经光谱确认的DES第5年超新星样本，我们在Ωm和ΩΛ上分别得到与DES合作组结果在0.12σ和0.23σ以内一致的约束，确立了该方法对真实巡天数据的可推广性。通过引入质量相关的尘埃消光定律来加入宿主星系相关系统误差，我们发现基于FPCA的摘要统计量隐含地编码了宿主相关系统误差，这表明在该框架内可能无需专门的宿主建模。结合其此前在测光分类中已证明的有效性，FPCA为统一的、数据驱动的流程提供了有吸引力的基础，这些流程能够为即将开展的宽视场测光巡天联合执行超新星分类和宇宙学推断。

## Abstract
Precision cosmology is a cornerstone of modern astronomy, and upcoming wide-field photometric surveys make it crucial to develop robust, data-driven inference methods that can handle complex survey systematics without relying on closed-form likelihoods. Simulation-Based Inference (SBI) meets this need by enabling forward simulations that encode complex survey characteristics. Previous SBI analyses in supernova cosmology have used SALT2 light-curve parameters as summary statistics; however, SALT2's two-basis spectral template imposes rigid modeling assumptions, motivating a more flexible representation. In this work, we present the first application of Functional Principal Component Analysis (FPCA) light-curve parameters as summary statistics for SBI-based cosmological inference under a non-flat ΛCDM model. On identically generated simulations, FPCA+SBI yields constraints comparable to both SALT2-based SBI and explicit-likelihood analyses, while providing more robust constraints on out-of-domain simulations than SALT2. Applying the model trained on LSST-like light curves to a spectroscopically confirmed DES Year 5 supernova sample, we recover constraints consistent with those of the DES collaboration to within 0.12 σ and 0.23 σ in Ωm and ΩΛ respectively, establishing the method's generalizability to real survey data. By introducing host-dependent systematics through a mass-dependent dust extinction law, we find FPCA-based summary statistics implicitly encode host-dependent systematics, suggesting dedicated host modeling may be unnecessary within this framework. Combined with its previously demonstrated effectiveness for photometric classification, FPCA offers a compelling foundation for unified, data-driven pipelines capable of jointly performing supernova classification and cosmological inference for upcoming wide-field photometric surveys.
